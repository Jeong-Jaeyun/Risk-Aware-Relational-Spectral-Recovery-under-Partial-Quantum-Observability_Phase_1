- Quantum Time.pdf
  Giovannetti, Lloyd, Maccone (2015). Quantum Time. arXiv:1504.04215. Foundational PW formulation directly invoked by Eqs. (1)-(3).

- Evolution without evolution, and without.pdf
  Marletto & Vedral (2016). Evolution without evolution, and without ambiguities. Resolves PW criticisms; supports the timeless construction.

- The Trinity of Relational Quantum Dynamics.pdf
  Hoehn et al. The Trinity of Relational Quantum Dynamics. Justifies clock-conditioning as one of three equivalent relational pictures.

- A relational solution to the problem of time in quantum mechanics and quantum.pdf
  Relational solution to the problem of time. Background motivation for using internal clock labels rather than external time.

- Time and classical equations of motion from quantum entanglement via the Page and Wootters mechanism with generalized coherent states.pdf
  PW with generalized coherent states. Justifies recovering classical time-like trajectories from clock-system entanglement.

- Robustness of the Page-Wootters construction across different pictures.pdf
  Robustness of PW construction. Supports treating PW as picture-independent; relevant to representation-robustness limitation.

- Non-linear equation of motion for Page-Wootters mechanism with interaction and quasi-ideal clocks.pdf
  Quasi-ideal clocks in PW. Directly relevant to your finite/clock-resolution discussion in S1 (clock recurrence, distinguishability).
